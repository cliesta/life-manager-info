Life Manager Calendar

A personal-use, AI-assisted life-management tool operated by its owner through OpenAI Codex.

Purpose

The tool helps its owner review commitments, organise tasks, plan appointments and identify relevant actions from documents.

Google access

  • Google Calendar: read calendars and manage authorised events.
  • Google Tasks: read and manage task lists' tasks.
  • Google Drive: discover and read files, and create or update files within the app's granted permissions.

Google account identity is used to connect the owner's account. Access is limited to the permissions granted through Google OAuth.

This is a private-use tool, not a public registration service. This website contains information about the tool only.
