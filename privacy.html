Privacy policy

Life Manager Calendar — updated 1 November 2026.

Purpose and data access

This tool is operated for its owner's personal use. It accesses Google account identity, calendar events, tasks, Drive file metadata and selected file contents to assist with personal planning and administration.

Processing and storage

Selected information is supplied to OpenAI through Codex for AI processing. OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policies and account settings govern that processing.

Google remains the authoritative store for its events, tasks and files. Selected context, source references, decisions and action records are retained in the owner's local life-management repository. Temporary document downloads are removed after inspection.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored separately from the repository on the owner's computer. They are not published on this website.

Use and disclosure

The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is used for the owner's requested life-management activities and processed through the services described above.

Retention and control

The owner can revoke Google access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised retrieval but does not automatically delete existing local notes or Codex chats. The owner manages those records and chats separately.

This website

This informational website does not provide Google sign-in or publish the owner's Google data. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub's privacy practices.

Contact

Contact the app owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
